Yago Studio

Yago Studio is an independent creator studio from Brazil that produces original short videos about video game news and curiosities, in several languages.

What the Yago Studio app does

The Yago Studio app is a publishing tool used only by the studio owner. It lets the owner connect the studio's own TikTok accounts and upload videos that the studio created itself, choosing the caption and privacy settings for each post. It does not access, collect or publish content from other users.

Content

Videos are made with original narration, original AI-generated visuals and music, and material used under licenses that allow reuse (such as Creative Commons Attribution), always with credit to the original authors. Yago Studio is fan content and is not affiliated with any game publisher.
