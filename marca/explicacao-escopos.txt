Yago Studio is a private publishing tool used only by its owner to post original short videos (game news and curiosities made by the studio) to the studio's own TikTok accounts, one per language.
Login Kit: the owner connects each account with Login with TikTok.
user.info.basic: shows the connected account's name and avatar so the owner picks the right account before posting.
Content Posting API (Direct Post) + video.publish: the owner reviews each video, writes the caption, chooses privacy level, comment/duet/stitch settings and content disclosure, then publishes.
video.upload: alternative flow that sends the video to TikTok drafts for final edits in the app.
video.list + user.info.stats: read views, likes, comments and shares of the studio's own videos and the follower count, shown in a private dashboard so the studio learns which formats work.
No data from other users is accessed; tokens stay on the owner's computer.
