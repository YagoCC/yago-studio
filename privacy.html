Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Yago Studio app ("the app") handles information when it is connected to a TikTok account. The app is a private publishing tool operated by Yago Studio, from Brazil, and is used only to publish videos to accounts owned by Yago Studio.

1. Information we access

When an account is connected through Login with TikTok, the app receives only what the authorized permissions allow:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): account identifier (open_id), display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).
  • Publishing results: the status and identifier of each video the app publishes, used to confirm that the upload worked.

The app does not access followers, messages, contacts, location, payment data or content created by other people.

2. How we use it

Exclusively to publish videos created by Yago Studio to the connected account and to verify that each post was published. We do not use this information for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and publishing records are stored only on the studio's own computer, are never published, and are not stored on third-party servers. Access is restricted to the studio owner.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, through its official API, to publish the videos.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is kept only while the account remains connected. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); after revocation, the stored tokens are deleted. Deletion requests can also be made through the contact below.

6. Children

The app is not intended for, and is not used by, anyone under 18.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/YagoCC/yago-studio/issues.
